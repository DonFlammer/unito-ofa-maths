#In brief

  • You have the maths OFA if you scored less than 5 points out of 20 in the Basic Mathematics section (Matematica di base) of the TOLC-S. Your online transcript (libretto) on MyUnito shows the activity “INT1475 OFA - MATEMATICA”.
  • To make it up you must take the “OFA Matematica” catch-up course (corso di riallineamento), in Italian, on www.ofa.unito.it and pass the in-person exam.
  • The exam, in Italian: 5 questions worth 2 points each, 45 minutes, pass mark 6/10, no calculator, on a computer in the Turing Lab (Laboratorio Turing).
  • You register for exam dates on MyUnito, for only one sitting per exam period (session); places are limited.
  • It must be passed within the first year: without it, you cannot have second-year exams recorded. You can, however, take first-year exams.

#Who has the OFA

Computer Science has open admission, but to enrol you must sit the TARM (the entry requirements test), which is taken through CISIA's TOLC-S. The TOLC-S has 55 questions in 6 sections (Basic Mathematics; Reasoning, comprehension, problems; Biology; Chemistry; Physics; Earth Sciences) plus 30 English questions, in 120 + 15 minutes.

For the degree programme, the test is passed with at least 5 points out of 20 in the Basic Mathematics section. Anyone who scores below that gets the maths OFA. There are no other thresholds.

Note Physics

If you scored less than 2/10 in the Physics section, the degree programme recommends (but does not require) that you take the physics catch-up course, also on www.ofa.unito.it. It is not an OFA and there is no exam to pass.

Sources: degree programme regulations (Regolamento didattico) for the 2026 cohort, article 2, paragraphs 7–11; the page Admission requirements, TARM and TOLC (Requisiti di ammissione, TARM e TOLC).

#What you need to do

Method From the transcript to the test
  1. Check your online transcript on MyUnito: if “INT1475 OFA - MATEMATICA” is there, you have the OFA.
  2. Log in to www.ofa.unito.it with your UniTo SCU credentials (the same as for MyUnito). You must already be enrolled and have active student status.
  3. Enrol in the “OFA Matematica” course: from the menu Corsi → Attività OFA (Courses → OFA activities), or directly here. Enrolment does not ask for a key.
  4. Study all the material of the 8 modules: the exam questions are based on everything there is on the platform. The notes on this site follow the same modules; how the course is organised, and which typos we found in its material, is explained on the page The official course.
  5. Register for an exam sitting on MyUnito, Esami (exams) section, as soon as registration opens: only one per exam period, limited places.
  6. Arrive on time at the Turing Lab with an identity document.

The degree programme recommends preparing straight away and aiming for the autumn sittings, so that you can move on faster with your studies: a solid grounding in the basics matters for everything you will study.

In the test What this means for how you study

With 45 minutes for 5 questions you have about 9 minutes per question, without a calculator. The questions are real exercises (an inequality, a system, a logarithm, a trigonometric equation…), not definitions learnt by heart. Practise doing the calculations by hand and against the clock: the mock tests have the same format. The questions are in Italian, like the whole course and its material: the page Italian for the OFA test lists the Italian words and phrasings you will meet.

  3. Corso di studio (degree programme): [0801L31] INFORMATICA.

#Where

The test takes place in the Turing Lab of the Department of Computer Science (Dipartimento di Informatica), via Pessinetto 12, Turin, on the raised ground floor (the same building as lecture rooms A and B). On Esse3 the room appears as “Lab Turing Informatica”. Map: OpenStreetMap.

#SpLD and disability

If you have a disability or an SpLD, when you register for the exam date you follow the same procedure as for other exams: the University's guidelines are on the page Support for students with SpLD (Supporto per studenti con DSA) and the documentation must be sent to the lecturers of the sitting (list on the degree programme's lecturers page). The extra time is one third: 1 hour instead of 45 minutes. A calculator can be allowed only for those who requested it within the deadlines.

#If you don't pass

  • You try again in the next exam period (one sitting per exam period).
  • The OFA must be passed within the first year of your programme. If you don't manage it, you cannot have second-year exams recorded until you pass it.
  • You can take first-year exams even while the OFA is still outstanding.
Rule 1 The rule for second-year exams

The 2026/27 Degree Programme Guide (Guida del corso di laurea) says that exams of a year after the first can be recorded only after recording at least 21 CFU (ECTS credits) of first-year exams and having passed the maths OFA (for those who did not reach the TOLC-S threshold).

The degree programme regulations (article 9) set the same 21 CFU threshold; the OFA is added to it for those who have one.

#Frequently asked questions

I scored 5/20 or more in Basic Mathematics: do I need to do anything? No, you don't have the OFA.

Can I make up the OFA by passing a first-year maths exam? The official instructions give only one way: the course on the platform and its exam. If your case is special (for example you have transferred or come from another degree), ask the OFA committee.

Is the course on the platform compulsory? The degree programme “requires attendance and passing” of the catch-up course, i.e. studying its material and passing its exam. In any case the questions are based on that material: study all of it.

Can I register for two sittings in the same exam period? No: one and only one.

Is the test on paper? No, on a computer on the exam platform, with your SCU credentials. You only get one sheet of scratch paper.

What happens with questions split into two parts? Each part is worth 1 point: you can get one even if you get the other wrong.
