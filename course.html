#In brief

  • The catch-up course (corso di riallineamento) is called “OFA Matematica” (OFA: additional learning requirement, obbligo formativo aggiuntivo) and is on www.ofa.unito.it (course number 20). You log in with your UniTo SCU credentials and enrol yourself, with no enrolment key.
  • It has 8 modules, divided into numbered units (1.1, 1.2, 2.1, …). For each unit there is a Libro (book), almost always an interactive “Esplora” (explore) worksheet, often a page of Applicazioni (applications), Esercizi and Soluzioni (exercises and solutions) as PDFs and an online Test; at the end of each module there is an end-of-module test.
  • The test questions are based on all the course material: the notes in this guide follow the same modules and the same units, but they do not replace it.
  • The material contains a few typos: you will find them at the bottom of this page, already verified. The notes have the correct version.

#The activities of each unit

ActivityWhat it isHow to use it
Librothe theory of the unit, divided into chapters, with worked examplesread it after the notes on the same topic, to get used to the course notation
Esploraan interactive Maple worksheet, with graphs and calculations on the topic of the unituse it to see what happens when a coefficient changes
Applicazionia page or a PDF with a real-world problem solved with the maths of the unitread it as an extra worked example
Esercizi and Soluzionitwo PDFs: first the exercises, then the solutions (often just the result)do them all by hand and check at the end
Testan online test (Maple T.A.) on the unit; at the end of the module there is the end-of-module testredo it until you get through it with no mistakes

The book shows the formulas as images: if a formula is hard to read, zoom in on the page.

#The modules and the units

#Module 1 · Language, sets, logic and numbers

Module section on the platform · notes: module 1

  • 1.1 Elementi di teoria degli insiemi e logica (elements of set theory and logic): Libro; Esplora “Gli insiemi” (sets); Applicazioni “Un rompicapo tra logica e vita quotidiana” (a puzzle between logic and everyday life; PDF); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Ulteriori esercizi di logica” (further logic exercises); Test 1.1.
  • 1.2 Numeri (numbers): Libro; Esplora “Operazioni tra numeri” (operations with numbers); Applicazioni “Dal linguaggio quotidiano alla simbologia matematica” (from everyday language to mathematical symbols); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Ulteriori esercizi sui numeri” (further exercises on numbers); Test 1.2.
  • Test modulo 1 (module 1 test).

#Module 2 · Polynomials and factorisation

Module section on the platform · notes: module 2

  • 2.1 Polinomi e fattorizzazione (polynomials and factorisation): Libro; Esplora “Polinomi e loro fattorizzazione” (polynomials and their factorisation); Applicazioni “La fattorizzazione di polinomi in matematica e non solo” (factorising polynomials, in mathematics and beyond); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Ulteriori esercizi sui polinomi” (further exercises on polynomials); Test 2.1.
  • Test modulo 2.

#Module 3 · First- and second-degree equations and inequalities, systems

Module section on the platform · notes: module 3

  • 3.1 Equazioni e disequazioni algebriche di 1° grado con una incognita (first-degree algebraic equations and inequalities in one unknown): Libro; Esplora “Le equazioni di 1° grado con una incognita” (first-degree equations in one unknown); Applicazioni “Un'equazione di 1° grado per esprimere l'età di tre fratelli” (a first-degree equation to express the ages of three siblings); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 3.1.
  • 3.2 Equazioni e disequazioni algebriche di 2° grado con una incognita (second-degree algebraic equations and inequalities in one unknown): Libro; Esplora “Le equazioni di 2° grado con una incognita” (second-degree equations in one unknown); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Applicazione di equazioni di 2° grado alla risoluzione di problemi” (applying second-degree equations to problem solving); Test 3.2.
  • 3.3 Sistemi di equazioni (systems of equations): Libro; Applicazione “Alla ricerca di un numero che soddisfi le richieste” (looking for a number that meets the requirements); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Sistemi lineari di equazioni” (linear systems of equations).
  • Test modulo 3.

#Module 4 · Rational, radical and absolute value equations and inequalities

Module section on the platform · notes: module 4

  • 4.1 Equazioni e disequazioni algebriche fratte (rational algebraic equations and inequalities): Libro; Esplora; Applicazione “Le equazioni fratte e la realtà: un binomio possibile?” (rational equations and reality: a possible pairing?); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 4.1.
  • 4.2 Equazioni e disequazioni irrazionali (radical equations and inequalities): Libro; Esplora “Le disequazioni irrazionali” (radical inequalities); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 4.2.
  • 4.3 Equazioni e disequazioni con valore assoluto (absolute value equations and inequalities): Libro; Esplora; Applicazione “Una disequazione con valore assoluto per esprimere la temperatura corporea” (an absolute value inequality to express body temperature); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 4.3.
  • Test finale modulo 4 (module 4 final test).

#Module 5 · Analytic geometry: lines and conics

Module section on the platform · notes: module 5

  • Introduzione: il piano cartesiano (introduction: the Cartesian plane; one page).
  • 5.1 La retta nel piano cartesiano (the straight line in the Cartesian plane): Libro; Esplora; Applicazioni “Quale parcheggio è più conveniente?” (which car park is cheaper?); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Ulteriori esercizi su piano cartesiano e retta” (further exercises on the Cartesian plane and the straight line); Test 5.1.
  • 5.2 Le coniche (conics): Libro (circle, parabola, ellipse, hyperbola); Esplora “La parabola nel piano cartesiano” (the parabola in the Cartesian plane); Applicazioni “La parabola per la risoluzione di disequazioni” (using the parabola to solve inequalities); Curiosità (fun fact) “Perché le coniche si chiamano ‘coniche’?” (why are conics called ‘conics’?); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 5.2.
  • Test fine modulo 5 (end-of-module test, module 5).

#Module 6 · Real functions of a real variable

Module section on the platform · notes: module 6

  • Nota introduttiva (introductory note; PDF).
  • 6.1 Definizione di funzione e principali caratteristiche (definition of a function and main properties): Libro; Esplora “Grafici di funzione” (graphs of functions); Applicazioni “Legame tra grafici ed equazioni” (the link between graphs and equations); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 6.1.
  • 6.2 Esempi di funzioni utili (examples of useful functions): Libro; Esplora “Il valore assoluto di una funzione” (the absolute value of a function); Applicazioni “Proporzionalità diretta e inversa in fisica” (direct and inverse proportionality in physics); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 6.2.
  • Test fine modulo 6.

#Module 7 · Exponentials and logarithms

Module section on the platform · notes: module 7

  • 7.1 Esponenziali e logaritmi (exponentials and logarithms): Libro; Nota (note) “La base eee, la base 101010” (the base eee, the base 101010); Esplora “La funzione esponenziale” (the exponential function) and “La funzione logaritmica” (the logarithmic function); Applicazioni “Esponenziali per modellizzare la realtà” (exponentials to model reality); Esercizi and Soluzioni “Ulteriori esercizi sui logaritmi” (further exercises on logarithms); Test 7.1.
  • 7.2 Equazioni e disequazioni esponenziali e logaritmiche (exponential and logarithmic equations and inequalities): Libro; Applicazioni “Il pH di una soluzione chimica” (the pH of a chemical solution); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 7.2.
  • Test fine modulo 7.

#Module 8 · Trigonometry

Module section on the platform · notes: module 8

  • Introduzione: circonferenza goniometrica e angoli (introduction: the unit circle and angles; one page).
  • 8.1 Funzioni trigonometriche (trigonometric functions): Libro; Esplora “La funzione trigonometrica seno” (the sine function) and “La funzione trigonometrica coseno” (the cosine function); Applicazioni “Legami con la fisica” (links with physics); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 8.1.
  • 8.2 Equazioni e disequazioni trigonometriche (trigonometric equations and inequalities): Libro; Applicazioni “Trigonometria e triangoli” (trigonometry and triangles); Esercizi and Soluzioni; Test 8.2.
  • Test fine modulo 8.

#The course notation

The notes use the same notation, so there are no surprises in the test:

  • trigonometric functions sin⁡x\sin xsinx, cos⁡x\cos xcosx, tan⁡x\tan xtanx and cotan⁡x\operatorname{cotan} xcotanx; natural logarithm ln⁡x\ln xlnx, logarithm to base aaa log⁡ax\log_a xloga​x;
  • intervals with round brackets (endpoint excluded) and square brackets (endpoint included): (−2,3](-2, 3](−2,3], (−∞,1)(-\infty, 1)(−∞,1);
  • in solutions: ∀x∈R\forall x \in \R∀x∈R (every real number), ∄x∈R\nexists x \in \R∄x∈R (no solution), ∨\vee∨ for “or” (oppure); “t.c.” for “such that” (tale che);
  • in trigonometry exercises the general solutions are written with k∈Zk \in \Zk∈Z, for example x=π4+kπx = \frac{\pi}{4} + k\pix=4π​+kπ.

#Typos in the material

Recalculating the course exercises to write the notes turned up a few typos. Each one was double-checked with symbolic computation and compared with the original page or PDF on 30 September 2026. The notes have the correct version. The list only includes the verified typos and is not a complete review of the material; in module 3 none were found. If you find others you can report them to the platform helpdesk.

#Module 1

  • Libro 1.1, proof by contradiction that if n2n^2n2 is even then nnn is even: with n=2p+1n = 2p + 1n=2p+1 we get n2=4p2+4p+1n^2 = 4p^2 + 4p + 1n2=4p2+4p+1, not 4p2+2p+14p^2 + 2p + 14p2+2p+1. The conclusion (n2n^2n2 is odd) is still correct.
  • Libro 1.1, example on how to state a theorem: two real numbers with the same absolute value are equal or opposite (a=ba = ba=b or a=−ba = -ba=−b), not “equal and opposite” (uguali ed opposti).
  • Libro 1.2, extension from Z\ZZ to Q\QQ: the equation ax=bax = bax=b has the solution x=bax = \frac{b}{a}x=ab​ when a≠0a \ne 0a=0; the book writes b≠0b \ne 0b=0.
  • Soluzioni 1.1, exercise 2, items iv and v: the negation of “all children are more than 6 years old” is “at least one child is at most 6 years old”, not “less than 6 years old” (a child of exactly 6 makes the original sentence false). In the same way, the negation of “there is a building with more than 10 floors” is “every building has at most 10 floors”.
  • Soluzioni 1.2, exercise 1: the largest four-digit number in which units plus tens equals hundreds plus thousands is 999999999999 (9+9=9+99 + 9 = 9 + 99+9=9+9), not 905490549054. Even requiring all the digits to be different, there are numbers larger than 905490549054, for example 968796879687.
  • Soluzioni 1.2, exercise 5: 278\frac{27}{8}827​ is not equal to 92\frac{9}{2}29​; an equivalent fraction is 276\frac{27}{6}627​.
