# Context for AIs

> Italian original: <https://github.com/DonFlammer/unito-ofa-matematica/blob/main/ai/README.md>

This folder collects in Markdown everything an AI needs to help you make up the **maths OFA** (additional learning requirement, *obbligo formativo aggiuntivo*) of the Computer Science degree programme (corso di laurea in Informatica) at the University of Turin **without redoing the research from scratch**: the official rules with sources, the dates, what the test is like, how the course on the OFA platform is organised (with the typos in its material already verified), the complete notes for the eight modules, an entry test, eight mock tests and a study plan. The pages of the site are generated from these same files, so the content is identical.

> **⚠️ Disclaimer.** Research and texts are based on public sources of the degree programme and of UniTo and on the material of the “OFA Matematica” course, consulted on the platform with a student's login. Every mathematical result was recomputed a second time, independently, with symbolic computation. They are accurate and sourced, but may contain errors or outdated data. **I, DonFlammer, publish this guide and take no responsibility**; anyone who uses them does so at their own risk and must check important information (rules, dates, registration) against the official sources. Full text: [../DISCLAIMER.md](../DISCLAIMER.md).

## Quick use

**A single file:** attach `_ALL_IN_ONE.md`, which contains all the others (it is regenerated at every update). It is large, about 590 KB, because it contains all the notes: if your AI does not accept it, use one of the two options below.

**Only rules and organisation:** for questions about who has the OFA, how to make it up, dates, registration and the study plan, `_ESSENTIALS.md` is enough (about 70 KB: instructions, student profile, rules, official course, Italian glossary and plan, without the notes).

**Or only the files you need:** always `ai-instructions.md` and your filled-in `student.md`, then the module you are studying (for example `modules/04-rational-radical-absolute-value.md`), or `entry-test.md` or `mock-tests.md` to practise.

A prompt to paste together with the files:

```text
I am attaching the context files for making up the maths OFA (Computer Science, University of Turin, academic year 2026/27).
Read ai-instructions.md first and follow its rules; use these files as your main source
instead of redoing the research, and tell me if anything seems out of date. My details are in student.md.
The official course and the test are in Italian: italian-glossary.md lists the Italian words and phrasings I will meet.
My request: <write your question here>
```

## File map

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `ai-instructions.md` | rules for the AI: what is verified and what is not, how to help (entry test, exercises, mock tests with the real scoring), how to read the files, how to write new content |
| `student.md` | profile to fill in: TOLC-S score, chosen sitting, hours per week, entry test result, how you want to be helped |
| `ofa-rules.md` | who has the OFA, the procedure, what the test is like, all the 2025/26 sittings with registrations and places, how to find the 2026/27 sittings on Esse3, venue, SpLD (specific learning disorders, DSA), what happens if you don't pass, contacts, sources |
| `official-course.md` | the “OFA Matematica” course on the platform: modules, units, activities of each unit, notation, verified typos in the material |
| `italian-glossary.md` | Italian for the OFA test: the key Italian terms of each module, how test questions are phrased, numbers and notation (only in this English version) |
| `study-plan.md` | method for studying a module, example of a week-by-week plan, calculations without a calculator, strategy for the 45 minutes |
| `modules/01-language-sets-numbers.md` … `modules/08-trigonometry.md` | the notes for the eight modules: theory, definitions and rules, methods, worked examples, common mistakes, graphs, exercises with solutions, check quizzes and checklists (134 exercises, 127 questions, 72 graphs in all) |
| `entry-test.md` | diagnostic test of 24 questions, 3 per module, with solutions |
| `mock-tests.md` | 8 mock tests in the format of the real test (5 questions, 45 minutes), with solutions and explanations |
| `FORMAT.md` | the syntax of the files (callouts, exercises, quizzes, graphs) |
| `_ESSENTIALS.md` | instructions, student profile, rules, official course, Italian glossary and plan merged into one file (generated by `tools/build.mjs`) |
| `_ALL_IN_ONE.md` | all the previous files merged (generated by `tools/build.mjs`) |
