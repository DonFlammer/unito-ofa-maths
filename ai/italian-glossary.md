The “OFA Matematica” course, the OFA platform (www.ofa.unito.it) and the test are all in Italian. This page lists the Italian words and phrases you will meet: first the key terms of each of the 8 modules, then how the test questions are phrased, and finally how numbers and symbols are written. Nouns are given with their Italian article (il, lo, la, l', i, gli, le) where it helps.

## How test questions are phrased

These are the phrasings used by the entry test and the mock tests of the Italian original of this guide; they are typical of Italian maths questions.

| Italian | English |
|---|---|
